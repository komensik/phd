This happens first because it involves a column I want to drop in the next cleaning step.

Assign Deserving & Power Blocks – redefine block using column names. Used the below to create block assignments for participants in A (n=900), B (n=898), and C (N=898) (to ultimately consolidate).

Explanation: Participants were sorted into 3 sections (ABC). For the deservingness and power questions, all 3 participant sections evaluated many of the same groups, but two of the three evaluated extra groups that participants in the other sections did not.

This shows up in the raw data:

  • there are 3 versions of the same variables – one corresponding to participants in section a, another in section b, another in section c.

Here, I am creating a block variable to keep track of what block each participant was sorted into, so that when I merge the disparate variables, it is still easy to know which group each participant was assigned to (thus, what groups they would have been prompted to evaluated and also important because it appears that the deserving questions for participants in block A were forced choice and snapped to increments of 10: not the case for B and C).

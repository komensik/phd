Did these people answer variables I use? AAPOR explicitly distinguishes survey breakoff from item nonresponse. Someone can reach the end but skip questions; someone can break off before the end but still provide usable answers to the crucial variables. AAPOR recommends coding unanswered questions distinctly from substantive answers such as “don’t know” and “prefer not to answer,” and emphasizes documenting partial responses rather than automatically treating them as invalid cases.

And the breakoff literature gives another reason not to equate finished == 0 with poor-quality respondent. Breakoff can be related to questionnaire design, burden, where a question occurs, and respondent characteristics. Peytchev found that breakoffs did not simply look like inattentive respondents.
